# Perfect Day
Original 2D adventure runner: guide Luna or Zara through one magical world, physically completing five tasks while the day shifts from morning to night.
Core mechanic: steer, jump, explore, interact, and collect memories; art style: warm hand-drawn cartoon shapes with dreamy parallax color.
